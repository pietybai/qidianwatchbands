Process

From brief to bulk

  1. Send your brief

    Model or lug width, material, hardware, logo and quantity.

  2. Review a sample

    We confirm construction and any new tooling before sampling.

  3. Approve the fit

    Check interface, colour and hardware on the physical sample.

  4. Confirm production

    Agree quantity, packaging and timing for the bulk run.

Questions buyers ask

What is the difference between OEM and private label?

OEM means we build to your design — your tooling, dimensions and hardware. Private label means an existing construction with your logo and packaging.

Do you charge for tooling?

New moulds carry a one-off cost that depends on construction, cavity count and finish. Where existing tooling fits, there is no tooling charge.

Can you handle packaging and barcodes?

Yes. Poly bags, printed sleeves, gift boxes, hang tags, barcode and FNSKU labels can be applied in production.

We have no design yet. Can you help?

Yes. Share the market and price position and we propose two or three constructions that fit, with their tooling implications.
