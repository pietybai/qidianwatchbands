What to include in your inquiry

A brief with these six points usually removes two rounds of email.

1. Band width

Lug width in mm, or the smartwatch model if the connector is moulded.

2. Length

Short, standard or long; or your wrist range in mm.

3. Material

Silicone, FKM, TPE or nylon — and any hardness preference.

4. Hardware

Pin buckle, deployment clasp, quick-release bars; steel grade and finish.

5. Quantity & colours

Target pieces per colour, and whether colours are fixed or open.

6. Market & packaging

Destination market, test requirements, retail or e-commerce packaging.

Programme questions

What is the difference between OEM and private label for watch straps?

OEM means we manufacture to your design — your tooling, dimensions, material and hardware. Private label means we produce an existing construction with your logo and packaging. Most first orders start as private label and move to OEM once the range proves itself.

What information do you need to quote a custom watch strap?

Band width, length, material, hardware type, logo method, target quantity per colour, target market, and any test or packaging requirement. Reference photos or drawings shorten the process considerably.

Do you charge for tooling?

New moulds carry a one-off tooling cost depending on construction, cavity count and surface finish. Where a design can use existing tooling there is no tooling charge. It is quoted separately so you can see what the mould adds.

Can you handle our packaging and barcodes?

Yes. Poly bags, printed sleeves, gift boxes, hang tags, barcode labels and FNSKU labelling can all be applied in production.

Do you work with new brands without an existing design?

Yes. Share the intended market and price position and we will propose two or three constructions that fit, with their tooling and unit-cost implications.

Fastest route to a quote: width, length, material, hardware, quantity per colour, market. Send those six and we reply with construction options and a quotation checklist.
