Royal Pop Conversion Band: Pocket Watch to Wristwatch

The Swatch × Audemars Piguet Royal Pop is a pocket watch, and most owners want it on the wrist. This silicone conversion band carries the bioceramic module in a 44 mm case cradle on a 20 mm strap, with a stainless steel pin buckle. Two variants cover both crown positions in the collection — 12 o'clock and 3 o'clock — so the case sits straight on the wrist whichever reference you own.

Which version do you need?

The collection is split by crown position. This changes how the watch sits on the wrist, so the cradle is built in two versions.

What the band does and does not include

Included

Silicone strap in two sections, 44 mm case cradle, stainless steel pin buckle and keepers, colour matched to the band.

Not included

The Royal Pop watch itself and its bioceramic module. We supply the band and cradle only; the watch must be sourced separately.

Replaces

The factory lanyard. The watch drops into the cradle and the assembly is worn as a wristwatch.

Brand owners: colour matching to your own palette, laser-etched or debossed branding on the band, and retail packaging are available. Minimums for custom colour and logo work are confirmed per project.

Buyer questions

Does this conversion band fit both crown positions of the Royal Pop?

Yes. Six references place the crown at 12 o'clock and two place it at 3 o'clock. We supply two variants of the band, one for each orientation, so the case sits correctly on the wrist whichever reference you own.

How do I order, and can I pay on the website?

There is no online checkout or payment on this site. Send your requirement by WhatsApp or email, we reply with the variant, price and lead time, and the order proceeds on a proforma invoice.

Is the watch or the bioceramic case included?

No. We supply the band and its case cradle only. The Royal Pop watch itself is not included and must be sourced separately.

What wrist sizes does the band cover?

From 5.6 to 8.5 inches, roughly 142 to 216 mm, using the 126.4 mm long section and 104.2 mm short section with the stainless steel pin buckle.

Can I order one piece to check the fit?

Yes. A single piece can be supplied as a sample. Bulk colourways start at 10 pieces per colour; custom colour matching or logo work is quoted per project.
