About

Watch Band Manufacturing in Shenzhen

Qidian Watch Bands makes watch bands and straps to order for brands, importers and independent sellers. We have been working in watch accessories since 2013, first as a trading partner and now producing silicone, TPU, nylon, ceramic and stainless steel bands under one roof. Our work is quoting and tooling to a customer's brief, not pushing a fixed catalogue.

What we actually do

Tooling and sampling

Moulds, colour lab dips and a physical sample before bulk. You approve a real part, not a rendering.

Production to your spec

Widths from 16 to 24mm, four material groups, hardware in stainless steel, packing to your requirement.

Private label

Debossed, printed or laser-etched branding, retail packaging, hang tags and barcode labels.
