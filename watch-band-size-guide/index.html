Watch Band Size Guide: Lug Width, Length and Wrist Size

Watch band size is two separate numbers: lug width, measured between the inside faces of the lugs in millimetres, and strap length, chosen from your wrist circumference. A 22mm band can be short, standard or long. This guide covers how to measure both, plus the widths we manufacture.

Sizing questions

How do you measure watch strap size?

Measure the distance between the inside faces of the two lugs, in millimetres — that is the lug width and it is the number used to size a band. Then measure wrist circumference to choose length. Lug width and length are separate specs: a 22mm band can be short, standard or long.

Is lug width measured in inches or millimetres?

Bands are almost always quoted in whole millimetres: 16, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22 or 24mm. A band listed as 0.87 inch is the same as 22mm.

What if my watch measures between two sizes?

Go to the smaller whole millimetre. A case measuring 21.8mm takes a 21mm band; a 22mm band will not fit between the lugs. Slightly undersized bands still mount correctly, oversized ones do not.

What length should a watch strap be?

Wrists under 165mm suit a short 70/110mm pair, 165–190mm suits standard 75/115mm to 80/120mm, and wrists over 190mm need a long 85/125mm or longer. Confirm against the buckle position you want.

Do smartwatch bands use the same sizing?

Not always. Some smartwatches use a proprietary moulded connector instead of a spring bar, so the band is sized by watch model rather than lug width. Standard-lug 20mm and 22mm watches use ordinary band sizing.

Buying for a brand, not a watch? Tell us the widths and lengths in your range and we will confirm which of our existing tools cover them — that is usually the difference between a tooling charge and none.
