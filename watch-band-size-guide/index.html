Sizing reference

Watch band size guide

Band size is two numbers: lug width, measured between the inside faces of the lugs, and strap length, chosen from wrist circumference. A 22 mm band can be short, standard or long.

Questions buyers ask

How do you measure watch strap size?

Measure between the inside faces of the two lugs in millimetres — that is the lug width used to size a band. Measure wrist circumference separately to choose length.

What if my watch measures between two sizes?

Go to the smaller whole millimetre. A 21.8 mm case takes a 21 mm band; a 22 mm band will not fit between the lugs.

What length do I need?

Under 165 mm wrist: short 70/110 mm. 165–190 mm: standard 75/115 to 80/120 mm. Over 190 mm: long 85/125 mm or more.

Do smartwatch bands use the same sizing?

Not always. Some use a moulded connector and are sized by watch model rather than lug width.
