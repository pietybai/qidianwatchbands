Before you write

Can you quote without a drawing?

Yes. Tell us the width, material and target market and we will propose two or three constructions with their tooling and unit-cost implications.

Do you send samples?

Yes. Sample cost and freight depend on whether the construction uses existing tooling or needs a new mould. We confirm both before anything is produced.

Is there a price list?

No. Unit cost depends on width, material, hardware, colour count and quantity, so it is quoted per programme rather than published.
