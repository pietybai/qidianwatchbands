Questions buyers ask

Can you quote without a drawing?

Yes. Tell us the width, material and market and we propose two or three constructions with their tooling implications.

Do you send samples?

Yes. Sample cost and freight depend on whether the construction uses existing tooling or needs a new mould.

Is there a price list?

No. Unit cost depends on width, material, hardware, colour count and quantity, so it is quoted per programme.
