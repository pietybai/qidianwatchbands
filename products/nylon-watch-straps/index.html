Questions buyers ask

Plain nylon or seatbelt webbing?

Plain nylon is thinner and draper, and suits one-piece and NATO-style straps. Seatbelt webbing is denser with a smoother face, holds shape better and resists abrasion.

Do you make one-piece and two-piece straps?

Both. One-piece passes under the case; two-piece uses a spring bar at each lug. Quick-release bars are available on 18–24 mm.

Can nylon be dyed to our colour?

Solid colours are matched to a Pantone reference with a woven swatch approved before bulk. Striped and jacquard layouts need a higher quantity.

Which widths are standard?

18, 20, 22 and 24 mm. Other widths depend on the weave and buckle size and are confirmed per project.

Lead time?

Samples in 7–10 working days after artwork approval. Bulk production in 15–25 working days.
