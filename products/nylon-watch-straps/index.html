Buyer questions

What is the difference between plain nylon and seatbelt webbing?

Plain nylon is thinner and softer and drapes closer to the wrist, suiting one-piece and NATO-style straps. Seatbelt webbing is a denser, tighter weave with a smoother face; it holds shape better and resists abrasion, so it is common on sport and field straps.

Do you make one-piece and two-piece nylon straps?

Yes. One-piece straps pass under the case; two-piece straps use a spring bar at each lug. Both are available with standard or quick-release bars.

Can nylon straps be dyed to a specific colour?

Solid colours are matched to your Pantone reference with a woven swatch approved before bulk. Striped and jacquard layouts are possible from a higher quantity because the weave plan is set up for the run.

Which widths do you produce in nylon?

18, 20, 22 and 24mm as standard. Other widths can be produced where the weave and buckle size allow, confirmed per project.
