Questions buyers ask

What is the minimum order?

One piece for a sample. Bulk orders start at 10 pieces per colour on stock programmes. Anything that needs new tooling is quoted per project, because the mould is part of the cost.

How long does sampling and bulk take?

Sampling runs 7–10 working days after the brief, colour and crown position are confirmed. Bulk production runs 15–25 working days from sample approval. Transit time depends on the shipping method you choose.

Which widths can you produce?

Silicone and nylon are tooled from 16 mm to 24 mm. Metal bracelets and ceramic programmes are sized to the case instead, so send the case reference or the lug measurement and we confirm the end link before quoting.

Are these bands compatible with my watch?

Only where the cradle or end link matches your case. Model names on this site describe compatibility, not affiliation. Send a dial photo or a reference number and we will confirm the fit before you order a sample.

Can you make a colour that is not listed?

Yes. Moulded silicone is matched to a Pantone reference, and plated metal is quoted per project. A physical swatch is approved before bulk, because plated and fired colours shift slightly between batches.
