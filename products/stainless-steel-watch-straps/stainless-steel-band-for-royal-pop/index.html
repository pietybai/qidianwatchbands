Fit and ordering

Does the bracelet fit a standard lug case?

These sets are cradle-integrated: the bracelet and cradle are one assembly cut to a specific case. If your case takes standard lugs, ask for the separate bracelet with straight end links and we quote that instead.

Which crown position do I need?

The cradle is cut for either the 12 o'clock or the 3 o'clock crown. Send the dial photo and we confirm the crown side before sampling.

Can we take the strap version on the same cradle?

Yes. Rubber and leather straps sit on the same cradle, so a programme can carry one bracelet and several straps without retooling the case part.

What is the minimum order?

One piece for a sample. Bulk from 10 pieces per colour; a new cradle is quoted per project.
