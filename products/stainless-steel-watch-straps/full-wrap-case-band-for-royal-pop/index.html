Fit

What this band fits

A full-wrap surround puts metal around the whole case instead of a cradle under it, so the watch sits in a case rather than on a mount. That changes the depth and the edge radius, which is why the pocket is cut per case and checked on a physical sample.

Fit and ordering

What exactly am I ordering?

The machined surround, plus a strap if you take one. The watch is supplied by you; we only quote the metal and the strap around it.

Does the surround change the case profile?

It adds a metal ring around the case and moves the strap to spring bars. Depth and edge radius are set in the sample, so the fit is checked physically before a bulk run.

Can we fit our own strap?

Yes, if the strap takes the same spring bar spacing. Tell us the strap width and we cut the surrounds to match.

What is the minimum order?

One piece for a sample. Bulk from 10 pieces per colour; a new surround is tooled per case and quoted as a project.
