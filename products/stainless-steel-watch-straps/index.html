Questions buyers ask

Link bracelet or milanese mesh?

A link bracelet is assembled from solid links with a folding clasp — heavier and more structured. Milanese mesh is a woven steel loop with continuous adjustment, thinner and finer.

Straight or curved end links?

Straight fits any standard lug width. Curved is shaped to the case profile so there is no gap, and needs the case model.

How is the bracelet sized?

Link bracelets are sized by removing or adding links; we set the count for your wrist range. Mesh adjusts through the clasp, so one size covers a wide range.

Which finishes?

Brushed, polished and mixed, plus PVD in gold tones, black and gunmetal, matched at the transition on two-tone pieces.

What is a full-wrap?

A full-wrap replaces the case surround as well as the band, rather than sitting between the lugs. It is heavier and more protective.
