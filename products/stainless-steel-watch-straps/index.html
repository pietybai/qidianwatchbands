Buyer questions

What is the difference between a steel link bracelet and milanese mesh?

A link bracelet is assembled from solid links with a folding clasp — heavier, more structured, sized by removing links. Milanese mesh is a woven steel loop with a sliding or magnetic clasp, thinner and continuously adjustable, with a finer look.

Do you make straight and curved end links?

Both. Straight end links fit any standard lug width. Curved end links are shaped to the case profile so the bracelet meets the case without a gap, which requires the case model and is quoted per programme.

How can the bracelet be sized?

Link bracelets are sized by removing or adding links; we set the link count for your target wrist range at production. Milanese mesh adjusts continuously through the clasp, so one size covers a wide wrist range.

Which finishes are available?

Brushed, polished and mixed finishes, plus PVD coating in gold tones, black and gunmetal, matched at the transition so two-tone constructions read as one piece.

What quantity can I start with?

A single piece can be supplied as a sample. Bulk colourways start at 10 pieces per colour; custom end links or a dedicated case programme are quoted per project.
