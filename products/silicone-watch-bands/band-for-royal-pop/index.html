Silicone · Conversion Band

Band for Royal Pop

A silicone conversion band that carries the Royal Pop bioceramic module in a case cradle and puts it on the wrist. The strap is 20 mm at the buckle, the cradle is 44 mm across, and the sections measure 126.4 mm and 104.2 mm to cover wrists from 142 to 216 mm. Two variants cover both crown positions in the collection, so the case sits straight whichever reference you own.

Buyer questions

Does this band fit both crown positions of the Royal Pop?

Yes. Six references place the crown at 12 o'clock and two place it at 3 o'clock. We supply two variants, one for each orientation, so the case sits correctly on the wrist whichever reference you own.

Is the watch included?

No. We supply the band and its case cradle only. The Royal Pop watch itself must be sourced separately.

What wrist sizes does the band cover?

From 5.6 to 8.5 inches, roughly 142 to 216 mm, using a 126.4 mm long section and a 104.2 mm short section with the stainless steel pin buckle.

How do I order?

There is no online checkout or payment on this site. Send the crown position, colour and quantity by WhatsApp or email, and the order proceeds on a proforma invoice.

Can I order one piece first?

Yes, a single piece can be supplied as a sample. Bulk colourways start at 10 pieces per colour; custom colour matching and logo work are quoted per project.
