Fit and ordering

Which cradle fits my reference?

The 3 o’clock version fits LAN BA (SSXO3L1OON) and OTG ROZ (SSXO3J1OON). The other six references use the 12 o’clock version. Include your reference or a dial photo for confirmation.

What comes with the band?

The strap, cradle, pin buckle and keepers replace the original lanyard. The buyer supplies the watch.

How is an order confirmed?

We confirm the variant, price and lead time by email or WhatsApp. After sample approval, we issue a proforma invoice on the agreed terms.
