Questions buyers ask

What shore hardness do you use for silicone bands?

Most bands run between Shore 45A and 60A. Softer compounds around 40A–45A feel more supple for dress and unisex bands, while 55A–70A holds shape on sport and dive straps.

Can you mould a connector for a specific watch?

Yes. We tool cradles and connectors for compact 38–42 mm watches, large 44–49 mm watches, and standard 20 mm and 22 mm quick-release systems.

Silicone or FKM?

Silicone gives a wider colour range, a softer feel and lower tooling cost. FKM resists oils and higher temperatures and is common on high-end dive straps, but has fewer colours.

What is the minimum order?

Samples from one piece. Bulk colourways start at 10 pieces per colour; new tooling is quoted per project.

How is the logo applied?

Debossed into the mould, screen printed, or laser etched on hardware. Debossing does not wear off because the mark is formed in the mould.
