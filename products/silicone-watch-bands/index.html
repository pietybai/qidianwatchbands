Technical questions

What shore hardness is best for a silicone watch band?

Most bands sit between Shore 45A and 60A. Softer compounds around 40A鈥?5A feel more supple for dress and unisex bands, while 55A鈥?0A holds shape better on sport and dive bands. Hardness test data for a specific batch is available on request.

Can you make a silicone band that fits a specific smartwatch?

Yes. We tool connectors for compact 38/40/41/42mm watches, large 44/45/46/49mm watches and standard 20mm/22mm quick-release systems. Send the exact model and we will confirm connector and lug width.

What is the difference between silicone and FKM rubber watch bands?

Silicone is softer, more colour-stable and cheaper to tool. FKM resists oils, fuels and higher temperatures better and is common on high-end dive straps, but costs more with fewer colour options. We produce both.

Do you offer quick-release spring bars?

Yes, on 18mm to 24mm bands, in stainless steel with 1.5mm or 1.8mm pin diameters.

How is a silicone band customised with our logo?

Debossing into the mould, silk-screen printing or laser etching. Debossing is most durable because the mark is formed during moulding; printing allows multicolour artwork; laser etching suits metal hardware.
