Fit

What this band fits

A pocket watch has no lugs, so the band has to carry the case in a cradle rather than hang from spring bars. Ceramic keeps the weight and the jewellery look of a bracelet while staying cool against the wrist, and the fired colour does not wear through at the edges where a band rubs most.

Fit and ordering

Which case does this band fit?

Every link set is cut for the Royal Pop case, so the cradle follows that case and its crown side. Send the model reference or a dial photo and we confirm the end link before sampling.

Is ceramic brittle?

It is hard and scratch resistant but less impact tolerant than steel, and a hard edge can chip it. We finish the link ends to reduce that risk, and a sample is the honest test.

All-ceramic or ceramic with silicone?

All-ceramic gives the full bracelet look and the most weight. The silicone version keeps the ceramic face with less weight for daily wear.

What is the minimum order?

One piece for a sample. Bulk colourways start at 10 pieces per colour. A new link geometry is tooled per project and quoted as part of the programme.
