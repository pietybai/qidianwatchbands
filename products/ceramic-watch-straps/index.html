Questions buyers ask

Why is a ceramic strap built for one case?

Ceramic links are finished to a fixed geometry, so the end link has to follow the case profile and lug spacing. That is why ceramic is quoted per case programme.

Can ceramic be colour matched?

White and black are the stable standard colours. Other tones are pigmented in the body and confirmed on a physical sample, because fired colour shifts between batches.

Is ceramic brittle?

It is hard and scratch resistant but less impact tolerant than steel and can chip on a hard edge. We finish link ends to reduce the risk.

Ceramic or ceramic with silicone?

All-ceramic gives the full bracelet look and the most weight. Ceramic with silicone is lighter and more comfortable for daily wear while keeping the ceramic face.

Minimum order?

One piece for sampling. Bulk colourways from 10 pieces per colour; a new link geometry is quoted per project.
