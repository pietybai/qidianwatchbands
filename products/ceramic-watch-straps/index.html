Ceramic Watch Straps

Ceramic link straps in polished single-colour and two-tone finishes. Because ceramic links are finished to a fixed geometry, the end link follows the case profile and lug spacing, so these are built per case programme rather than as a universal fit. White and black are the stable standard colours; other tones are matched against a physical sample.

Buyer questions

Why do ceramic straps have to be built for a specific case?

Ceramic links are made and finished to a fixed geometry, so the end link has to follow the case profile and lug spacing. That is why ceramic straps are quoted per case programme rather than as a universal fit.

Can ceramic be colour matched?

White and black are the stable standard colours. Other tones are produced by pigmenting the ceramic body and confirmed against a physical sample, because fired colour shifts slightly between batches.

Is ceramic brittle?

Ceramic is hard and scratch resistant but less impact tolerant than steel and can chip if dropped onto a hard edge. We finish edges and link ends to reduce the risk and can supply a steel alternative for high-impact use.

Do you make two-tone ceramic and steel straps?

Yes. Two-tone constructions combine ceramic links with stainless steel, or a ceramic body with steel end links, matched by finish so the transition reads as one piece.

What quantity can I start with?

A single piece can be supplied as a sample. Bulk colourways start at 10 pieces per colour; new link geometry or a dedicated case programme is quoted per project.
