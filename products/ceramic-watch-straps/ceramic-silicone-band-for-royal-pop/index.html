Fit

What this band fits

Holding a pocket watch on the wrist means the band takes the whole load at one point. Ceramic alone is stiff there, so the moulded inner takes the flex while the ceramic face keeps the finish, and the assembly stays light enough for daily wear.

Fit and ordering

How is this different from the all-ceramic band?

The outer face is still ceramic; the inner is moulded. That removes most of the weight and lets the band flex, which matters on smaller wrists and on a case that sits high.

Are both materials visible in wear?

The ceramic carries the visible face and edges. The inner only shows at the wrist side and at the two-tone split, which is part of the design rather than a hidden compromise.

Which case does it fit?

One case profile per tooling. Send the dial photo and we confirm the crown side before sampling.

What is the minimum order?

One piece for a sample. Bulk colourways from 10 pieces per colour; new tooling is quoted per project.
