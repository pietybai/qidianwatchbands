Product Range

Watch Bands, Grouped by Material

Every band we make sits under one of four material groups, because the material decides the tooling, the finish, the price bracket and the tests that apply. Pick a group to see the constructions, widths and hardware available in it.

Silicone & TPU Bands

One-piece and two-piece silicone, TPU and FKM bands from 16 to 24mm, with moulded smartwatch connectors, quick-release bars and logo debossing. This is our main line.

View silicone & TPU →

Nylon & Woven Bands

Plain nylon, seatbelt weave, herringbone and hook-and-loop straps in 18 to 24mm, with spring bar or quick-release fitting.

Ask about nylon →

Metal & Ceramic Bands

Stainless steel, milanese mesh, ceramic links and rhinestone-set alloy bands built for specific case programmes, in single colour or two-tone finishes.

Ask about metal & ceramic →

Bicep & Arm Bands

Upper-arm bands for fitness trackers and wearables, in silicone, TPU, woven nylon and elastic, with hook-and-loop or buckle fastening.

Ask about arm bands →

Not sure which group fits your product? Send the material and the device it goes on. We confirm the construction, tooling position and minimum quantity for that specific item rather than quoting a generic figure.
