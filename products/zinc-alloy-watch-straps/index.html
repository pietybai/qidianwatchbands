Buyer questions

Are the rhinestones glued or set into the alloy?

The stones are set into the cast alloy body rather than glued onto a flat surface. Setting into the metal keeps the row aligned and holds each stone mechanically, which is what determines how the piece survives daily wear.

How many finishes are available?

Twelve stock finishes: black, silver, rose gold, yellow gold tone, pink, light pink, red, green, mint green, light blue, navy blue and grey blue. Other tones are plated to a Pantone reference and confirmed on a physical sample.

Does the plating wear off?

Any plated alloy wears with abrasion, and wear shows first on edges that rub a desk or the clasp. Plating thickness is confirmed per order, and we can quote a stronger finish where the piece is worn daily.

Is the watch included?

No. The listing covers the band and its case cradle only. The watch module must be sourced separately.

What quantity can I start with?

One piece can be supplied as a sample. Bulk colourways start at 10 pieces per colour; a new cradle geometry or custom stone layout is quoted per project.
