Questions buyers ask

Are the stones glued or set into the alloy?

Set into the cast body rather than glued onto a flat surface. Setting into the metal keeps the row aligned and holds each stone mechanically.

How many finishes?

Twelve stock finishes: black, silver, rose gold, yellow gold tone, pink, light pink, red, green, mint green, light blue, navy blue and grey blue. Other tones are plated to a Pantone reference.

Does the plating wear?

Any plated alloy wears with abrasion, showing first on edges that rub a desk or the clasp. Plating thickness is confirmed per order.

Is the watch included?

No. The programme covers the band and its case cradle only.

Minimum order?

One piece for sampling. Bulk colourways from 10 pieces per colour; a new cradle or stone layout is quoted per project.
