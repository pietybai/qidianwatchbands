Fit

What this band fits

The stones sit in the casting rather than on top of it, so a row cannot drift where the band bends at the wrist — which is the failure a glued row shows first. Twelve stock finishes cover the colourways a retail programme usually asks for.

Fit and ordering

Are the stones glued on?

No. They are set into the casting, so the row keeps its alignment and a stone cannot lift at one edge the way a glued row does.

How many colours can we choose?

Twelve finishes are held for sampling. Anything outside them is plated to a Pantone reference and quoted per project.

Does the band fit my watch?

The octagonal cradle is built to one case, so send the reference or a dial photo. The band is not universal.

What is the minimum order?

One piece for a sample. Bulk from 10 pieces per colour; a new cradle is quoted per project.
