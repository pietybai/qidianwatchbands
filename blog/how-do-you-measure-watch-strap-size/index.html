Size guide

How do you measure watch strap size?

Measure the gap between the inside faces of the two lugs in millimetres: that is the band width, usually 16 mm to 24 mm. Then measure wrist circumference to choose length — under 165 mm takes 70/110 mm, 165–190 mm takes 75/115 to 80/120 mm, over 190 mm takes 85/125 mm.

Last updated 27 November 2026 · Qidian Watch Bands, Shenzhen

Two numbers, measured separately

Band size is not one measurement. Lug width decides whether the band fits the watch; strap length decides whether it fits the wrist. Buyers regularly conflate them and order a 22 mm band for a 20 mm case. Measure both before quoting.

How to measure lug width

Use a caliper across the gap between the two lugs, on the inside faces, with the old band removed. Measure to 0.5 mm. A ruler held at an angle reads 1–2 mm wide, which is the most common cause of a returned band.

If the case measures 21.8 mm, order 21 mm. Bands are not compressible: a 22 mm band will not seat in a 21 mm gap, and forcing it scratches the lugs. If the reading falls between two whole millimetres, always take the smaller one.

How to choose strap length

Wrap a tape around the wrist where the watch sits, over the wrist bone, and read the circumference. Then use the chart below. A band that is too long leaves a tail past the keeper; too short and the buckle cannot reach the third hole.

When lug width does not apply

Smartwatches and trackers often use a moulded connector instead of spring bars, and those are sized by watch model rather than by millimetres. The same is true of a full-wrap surround, which is cut for one case shape. Ask for the model, not the width, in those cases.

Four mistakes that cause returns

Measuring the case width instead of the lug gap. Reading a ruler at an angle. Assuming a 22 mm band fits a 21.8 mm case. Forgetting that a thick nylon band needs 2 mm of clearance under a slim case. Each of these is avoidable with a caliper and one photo of the case back.

Questions buyers ask

How do you measure watch strap size?

Remove the old band and measure the gap between the inside faces of the two lugs with a caliper. That measurement in millimetres is the band width. Measure wrist circumference separately with a tape to choose length.

What if my watch measures between two sizes?

Take the smaller whole millimetre. A 21.8 mm case takes a 21 mm band. A 22 mm band will not fit into a 21 mm gap, and forcing it damages the lugs.

What length strap do I need for a 180 mm wrist?

A 75/115 mm or 80/120 mm band covers a 180 mm wrist. The longer pair leaves more tail past the keeper; the shorter sits closer to the buckle.

Do I measure the case or the lugs?

The lugs. Case width is the diameter of the watch head and has nothing to do with band size. The lug gap is the distance between the two horns that hold the spring bar.

Do smartwatch bands use lug width?

Not usually. Models with a moulded connector are sized by watch model. Where the watch has standard lugs, the same 16–24 mm rule applies.
