Watch band guides

Three guides on choosing and sizing watch bands for wholesale buyers: the six constructions, how to measure lug width and strap length, and how nylon compares with leather over a year of wear.

Last updated 27 November 2026 · Qidian Watch Bands, Shenzhen

How to use these guides

Each guide answers one question a buyer asks before ordering: which construction suits the watch, what size goes on the specification sheet, and how a textile band behaves next to a leather one. They are written from the factory side, so the numbers are the ones that decide tooling, sampling and bulk timing rather than the ones used in retail copy.

If a question is not covered here, send the case width and a photo of the case back. Every band we make runs 16–24 mm at the lugs, and the lug gap decides the rest. Sampling takes 7–10 working days, bulk 15–25 working days, and bulk starts at 10 pieces per colour.
