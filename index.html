What we manufacture

Three material lines cover most brand programmes. Each one is produced in-house, so tooling, colour matching and packaging stay under one roof.

Silicone Watch Bands

One-piece and two-piece silicone in 16–24mm, plus shaped connectors for smartwatch models. Shore 40A–70A, matte or soft-touch finish, printed or debossed logos.

Leather Watch Straps

Genuine and top-grain leather, stitched or hand-finished, with matched hardware. Offered as part of our custom strap programme.

Nylon & Fabric Straps

How an order runs

  1. Brief

    Send drawings, reference samples or a target look, plus quantity and market.

  2. Sample

    We confirm tooling and material, then produce a sample in 7–10 working days.

  3. Approval

    Colour, hardness, hardware and logo checked against your artwork.

  4. Production

    Bulk run in 15–25 working days, then packed to your requirement.

Buyer questions

What is your minimum order quantity for custom watch bands?

It depends on the style, not on a single company-wide number. Selected stock styles can ship from a single piece as a sample; stock colourways start at 10 pieces per colour; new moulds are quoted per project with the tooling cost and minimum quantity confirmed together. Tell us the style and target quantity and we will confirm the exact figure for that item.

Can you produce watch bands with our logo?

Yes. Debossed, embossed, printed and laser-etched logos on silicone, leather and stainless hardware, plus buckle engraving and private label packaging.

Which watch band widths do you manufacture?

16, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22 and 24mm lug widths, plus dedicated connectors for 38/40/41/42mm and 44/45/46/49mm smartwatches and 20mm/22mm quick-release systems.

How long does a custom watch band order take?

Samples in 7–10 working days after artwork approval. Bulk production in 15–25 working days depending on quantity, colours and hardware.

Not sure where to start? Send your target market, band width, quantity and whether you need a logo or custom packaging. We reply with a material recommendation and a quotation checklist — no price list required up front.
